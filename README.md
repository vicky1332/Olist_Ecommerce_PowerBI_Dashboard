## 📊 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive **Power BI Business Intelligence dashboard** developed to analyze the performance of the Olist e-commerce business.

The dashboard transforms raw e-commerce data into actionable business insights across **overall business performance, customer behavior, geographic distribution, operational efficiency, delivery performance, and product performance**.

The objective of this project is to demonstrate how data analytics and business intelligence can be used to identify trends, measure key performance indicators (KPIs), and support data-driven decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The dashboard was developed to answer key business questions such as:

- How is the overall e-commerce business performing?
- How are customers distributed geographically?
- What is the customer mix between new and returning customers?
- Which customer segments contribute more based on spending?
- Which states generate higher order volumes?
- How does delivery performance vary over time?
- Which states have higher average delivery times?
- How does freight value vary across customer states?
- What proportion of deliveries are completed on time versus late?
- Which product categories generate the highest revenue?
- Which product categories have the highest order volume?
- How does product-category revenue change over time?
- Which product categories contribute most to overall revenue?

# 📌 Dashboard Pages

The Power BI report consists of four analytical pages.

## 1. Business Overview

The Overview page provides a high-level view of the business and its key performance indicators.

It focuses on:

- Overall order volume
- Revenue performance
- Customer activity
- Order and delivery trends
- Key business KPIs
- Overall business performance

This page is designed to provide management with a quick understanding of the current business position before moving into detailed analysis.

---

## 2. Customer & Geographic Analysis

This page focuses on understanding customer behavior and geographic patterns.

### Key Visualizations

- New vs Returning Customers
- Customer Segment by Spend
- Average Order Value by Customer State
- Customer Distribution by State
- Orders by Customer State

### Key Insights

The analysis highlights a strong concentration of customers and orders within specific Brazilian states.

São Paulo (SP) represents a particularly significant share of order activity, making it an important market for the business.

The customer spend segmentation also helps distinguish customers based on their purchasing contribution and provides a useful foundation for customer-focused business strategies.

---

## 3. Operations & Delivery Analysis

This page evaluates operational efficiency and delivery performance.

### Key Visualizations

- Estimated vs Actual Delivery Time
- Average Delivery Time by Customer State
- Average Freight Value by Customer State
- On-Time vs Late Deliveries
- Monthly Order Volume

### Key Insights

The delivery analysis shows that approximately **91.89% of deliveries were completed on time**, while approximately **8.11% were late**.

The comparison between estimated and actual delivery time provides visibility into delivery performance and helps identify periods where actual delivery duration differs significantly from expectations.

The state-level delivery analysis also highlights geographical differences in average delivery time.

Monthly order-volume analysis shows substantial growth in order activity over the observed period, followed by fluctuations in monthly demand.

---

## 4. Product Performance Analysis

The Product Performance page evaluates product-category performance from multiple business perspectives.

### Key Visualizations

- Product Category by Revenue
- Product Category by Order Volume
- Product Category by Average Review Score
- Monthly Revenue by Product Category
- Revenue Contribution by Top 5 Product Categories

### Key Insights

The analysis identifies the product categories that contribute most significantly to revenue and order volume.

The revenue analysis shows strong performance from categories such as:

- Health & Beauty
- Watches & Gifts
- Bed & Bath Table
- Sports & Leisure
- Computers & Accessories

The Top 5 revenue contribution analysis provides a clearer view of the categories that have the greatest impact on overall revenue.

The monthly category-level analysis also helps identify changes in product demand and revenue performance over time.

---

# 📈 Key Business Insights

Some of the major insights identified through the dashboard include:

### 🚚 Strong Delivery Performance

Approximately **91.89% of deliveries were completed on time**, while approximately **8.11% were late**.

This indicates generally strong delivery performance while also highlighting an opportunity to investigate the causes of delayed deliveries.

### 🌎 Geographic Concentration

Order activity is highly concentrated in certain Brazilian states, with **São Paulo (SP)** being the dominant state in terms of order volume.

This geographic concentration can be useful for decisions related to logistics, fulfillment, inventory positioning, and regional marketing.

### 👥 Customer Segmentation

Customers were segmented based on their total spending into:

- Low
- Medium
- High
- VIP

This segmentation can support targeted customer strategies such as personalized marketing, retention programs, and customer-value analysis.

### 📦 Product Category Performance

Revenue and order-volume analysis reveals significant differences between product categories.

Categories such as **Health & Beauty, Watches & Gifts, Bed & Bath Table, Sports & Leisure, and Computers & Accessories** demonstrate strong business contribution.

### 📅 Order Growth Over Time

Monthly order-volume analysis indicates substantial growth in order activity throughout the observed period, followed by variations in demand across subsequent months.

This provides useful visibility into demand patterns and can support operational and inventory planning.
